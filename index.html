Where Are NSW Government Schools Located?

This interactive symbol map displays the geographic locations of NSW government schools using the October 2026 school location dataset. Each circle represents one school, and colours distinguish different school types. Use the dropdown to filter schools by type. Hover over a circle to view the school name, location and latest reported enrolment.

Data source: NSW Department of Education, NSW Public Schools Master Dataset (2026). This map provides geographic context and does not represent language-specific enrolments.

Which NSW Areas Have the Most Government Schools?

This proportional symbol map compares the number of NSW government schools across local government areas (LGAs). Larger circles represent LGAs with more schools. Each circle is positioned at the average location of schools within that LGA. Use the school-type dropdown to explore different types of schools, and adjust the minimum-school slider to focus on areas with larger school counts.

Data source: NSW Department of Education, NSW Public Schools Master Dataset (2026). Circle sizes represent school counts, not language enrolments or population-adjusted density.

How Do Government School Counts Vary Across NSW?

This choropleth map shows the number of NSW government schools in each Local Government Area. Hover over an area to view its name and school count.

Source: NSW Government school locations and LGA boundaries. This map shows school counts, not school density or language-specific enrolments. Two schools with unassigned LGA boundaries are excluded.
